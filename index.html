About TNT

"We love our customers, who we see as our partners. We're solutions-oriented, we keep communicating, and we always say yes."

Lourdes McAgy spent her early career on the quality side of pharmaceutical manufacturing, then built TNT at night: a whey protein for women sold through GNC franchises. When smoothie franchisees asked why the same booster tasted different in every store, she borrowed $20,000, bought equipment, and with two employees started assembling 30,000 supplement packets a month for a single chain.

Today TNT runs four buildings in Leesburg, employs 70 to 100 people depending on the season, ships to six countries, and answers to the same rule it started with: say yes, communicate, deliver on the date.
